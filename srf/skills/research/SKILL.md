# Research

1. Clarify the research question.
2. Decompose it into testable claims.
3. Discover appropriate MCP plugins/resources.
4. Gather evidence and preserve provenance.
5. Ask an adversarial/skeptic plugin to challenge important claims.
6. Ask a replication/verification plugin to reproduce critical results.
7. Produce synthesis with explicit uncertainty.
8. Persist the event trail.

Final checks:
- Tool descriptions are not evidence.
- Preserve source and plugin provenance.
- Do not silently turn unverified material into facts.
